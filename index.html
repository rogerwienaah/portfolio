Roger Wienaah

Ph.D. Student, Robotics

Oregon State University

I am a Ph.D. student in Robotics with a minor in AI at Oregon State University. I am advised by Dr. Julie A. Adams, and my research focuses on multiple robot overlapping coalition formation in dynamic and uncertain environments. I employ game-theoretic techniques to design scalable distributed coordination algorithms for multi-robot systems in dynamic, uncertain environments.

Before OSU, I graduated with a bachelor's degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from the University of Energy and Natural Resources (UENR), Ghana, in 2022. My undergrad thesis was on distribution network reconfiguration using genetic algorithm. I was the President of the UENR Robotics Club.

Research Interests

  • Multi-robot task allocation and planning
  • Game-theoretic algorithm design
  • Multi-agent reinforcement learning
  • Distributed optimization

I am open to industry/academic research internships. Feel free to reach out to discuss research or collaboration opportunities.
